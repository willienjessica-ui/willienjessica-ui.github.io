Vendor Operating Model

  • 1Apply for a storefrontVendors register interest, identify their district, and explain what they plan to sell.
  • 2Pass mall reviewMorris Lane approves fit, product standards, support expectations, and rule alignment before a store goes live.
  • 3Operate the storeApproved vendors manage their own products, inventory, pricing, customer operations, and fulfillment.
  • 4Receive Debbie receiptsTransactions, product updates, disputes, and rule decisions produce auditable governance records.

World Rules

What DNA-Stamped Means

DNA-stamped products carry provenance from the source. Morris Lane digital goods are built to show where they came from, who created them, what license applies, and whether the file is authentic.

Verified Source

Every digital file can carry source and authorship records that buyers can inspect before they pay.

Authentic Files

Buyers trust they are receiving official Morris Lane files, not copied junk from a resale aggregator.

License Clarity

Each product shows personal, commercial, or vendor-use rights so customers always know what they bought.

Debbie Receipts

Debbie creates audit records for transactions, product updates, disputes, and marketplace rule decisions.
